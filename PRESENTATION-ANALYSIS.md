## Visual language

The actual media use botanical greens, pale yellow highlights, white backgrounds, delicate line drawings, and real butterfly imagery. The Office theme contains generic blue/orange defaults, but these are not the meaningful visual identity. The redesign retains forest green, leaf green and pale yellow, with restrained rose/blue accents, generous white space and Hebrew Assistant/Heebo typography instead of the presentation's generic Office font settings.

Six embedded raster images were extracted. Several are decorative slide backgrounds with empty text placeholders, crop marks, or a "Gemini Notebook" watermark. These are not displayed as website sections. Only the actual photograph, individual lifecycle photos, and garden illustration are reused after cropping. Diagram elements are rebuilt as React components.

All six speaker-note parts were inspected; they contain slide-number placeholders only, with no additional narrative content. No chart parts were referenced by any slide.

## Content integrity

No numeric data charts, measured statistics, percentages, budgets, dates, area estimates, contact channels, or confirmed funding are present. Therefore the site does not invent KPI cards or charts. "Gemini Notebook" is an asset-generation watermark, not project content or branding. The main experience remains Hebrew and RTL. Plant-role labels are explanatory classifications, not additional species or planting commitments.

## Experience structure

The current experience is a sixteen-scene web presentation with forward content clicks and a dedicated previous-slide button:

1. Photographic opening and project identity.
2. Four vision pillars, all visible without selecting a node.
3. Scientific foundation and Dovi Benyamini's book.
4. Therapeutic gardening and metamorphosis.
5. Municipal precedents in Bat Yam and Mateh Yehuda.
6. Collecting and raising swallowtail caterpillars.
7. The chrysalis, waiting and regulation.
8. Hatching, flight and both original personal quotations.
9. Planned accessible space, seating, research tables and handwashing.
10. Ecological beds with all five selected plants grouped by role.
11. Planning and approvals.
12. Funding and partners.
13. Detailed planning and community construction.
14. Operation and ongoing sustainability.
15. All three council requests and community partners.
16. Closing invitation and original team attribution.

Content clicks and taps advance one scene. Horizontal keyboard arrows follow Hebrew reading direction: ArrowLeft advances and ArrowRight goes back. Other presentation keys retain their forward behavior. At the user's later request, a dedicated previous-slide button was added; it is disabled on the first scene. Progress segments fill from right to left and retreat when moving back. There is no automatic timed advance, looping, scroll dependency, hidden detail, or internal interaction. Fullscreen is optional and does not advance the presentation. Native scene transitions, staged content reveals, animated diagram motifs, photography and butterfly flight make the narrative expressive; reduced-motion users receive static readable scenes.
